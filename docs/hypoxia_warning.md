LIFE-SAFETY CRITICAL WARNING: NITROGEN ASPHYXIATION BEHAVIOR
============================================================

The Illusion of Sobriety and Hypoxia-Induced Anosognosia
--------------------------------------------------------
When liquid nitrogen expands and forces ambient oxygen levels below 19.5 percent, the human brain experiences rapid frontal lobe failure. Victims suffer from hypoxia-induced anosognosia, meaning they possess a total inability to recognize their own medical impairment. 

Do not expect victims to ask for help or realize they are suffocating.

Documented Behavioral Symptoms
------------------------------
Because the brain cannot process the lack of oxygen, it triggers severe chemical intoxication and primitive survival responses. Responding engineers must be prepared to encounter the following:

* Acute Paranoia: Victims frequently experience extreme fight-or-flight panic attacks.
* Persecutory Delusions: Individuals may accuse coworkers of drugging them or targeting them, as they cannot comprehend why they suddenly feel dizzy and confused.
* False Euphoria: Victims may appear completely normal, giggly, or heavily intoxicated while actively suffocating.
* Combativeness: Disoriented personnel may aggressively fight rescuers attempting to pull them to safety.

Emergency Action Protocol
-------------------------
* Evacuate Instantly: If the safety array triggers, clear the corridor immediately.
* Do Not Negotiate: Never argue with disoriented or accusatory personnel inside the leak zone. Force or drag them into fresh air if it is safe to do so.
* Protect Rescuers: Never enter a tripped zone to save someone without a dedicated, independent respiratory air supply. Multiple casualties occur when rescuers succumb to the same invisible gas pool.
